# 🏥 Smart Dengue Patient Monitoring System
### Integrated Smart Monitoring Unit for Adult Dengue Patient Care in Sri Lankan Hospitals

🏆 **3rd Runner-Up – SPARK Challenge 2025/26**

An AI and IoT-powered healthcare platform designed to automate dengue patient monitoring, reduce the workload of healthcare professionals, and improve clinical decision-making through real-time data collection, intelligent alerts, and centralized patient management.

---

## 📌 Overview

Dengue fever continues to place a significant burden on Sri Lanka's healthcare system, especially during seasonal outbreaks where hospitals face overcrowding and limited medical resources.

Current monitoring of dengue patients is largely manual, requiring nurses to repeatedly measure vital signs, record observations on paper charts, and manually analyze patient trends. This process is time-consuming, error-prone, and can delay the identification of critical warning signs.

The **Smart Dengue Patient Monitoring System** addresses these challenges by integrating IoT devices, computer vision, cloud technologies, and intelligent software into a single platform that continuously monitors patients and provides real-time insights to healthcare professionals.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Hospital staff currently face several challenges:

- Manual measurement of patient vital signs
- Repetitive paper-based data recording
- High nurse workload during dengue outbreaks
- Difficulty identifying patient deterioration early
- Lack of centralized digital patient records
- Limited real-time visualization of patient trends

These challenges increase the possibility of delayed interventions during the critical phase of dengue infection.

---

# 💡 Solution

The proposed system combines a Raspberry Pi–based monitoring device with a secure web platform to automate patient monitoring and digital health record management.

The platform continuously collects physiological data, stores it in a centralized database, analyzes trends, and generates intelligent alerts whenever abnormal conditions are detected.

Healthcare professionals can monitor multiple patients through a real-time dashboard while maintaining complete digital medical records.
